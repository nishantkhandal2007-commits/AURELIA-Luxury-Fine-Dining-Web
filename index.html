DECADE OF CULINARY CRAFT

OUR HERITAGE

A JOURNEY THROUGH TUSCAN GASTRONOMY

Aurelia began with a simple philosophy: honor centuries-old Italian cooking techniques while sourcing regional organic produce. Every pasta strand is bronze-die extruded in our open kitchen, and our sour ferment rests for 48 hours before baking in 900°F oak ovens.

Whether you join us for an intimate anniversary dinner, a celebratory family gathering, or a glass of vintage Chianti at the bar, we welcome you to our table.
